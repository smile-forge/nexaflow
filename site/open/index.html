Open anything

One window that already knows what a file is.

Double-click and it lands in the right tool — not a generic preview, but a viewer that understands the format. A DICOM study opens as a study, with its series and its window/level. An executable opens as headers, sections, imports and exports. An archive opens as a folder you can walk into, open files inside, edit, and write back.

Type ? in the input bar and you search whatever you are looking at — a log, a spreadsheet, a registry hive, your processes — each page answering in the way that suits it, and ?/pattern/ is a regular expression across all of them.
