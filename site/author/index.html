Author & draw

A Markdown editor that draws — natively, on your machine, with no browser anywhere in the picture.

Formulas are not pictures: put a caret in one and edit it. Twenty-four Mermaid diagram types are drawn on the canvas, alongside QR codes, five barcode families, chemical structures from SMILES strings, shaped word clouds, and correlation plots over your own data.

Every figure on these pages was drawn by the renderer that ships in the app.

Diagrams for what happens, in what order, and who it is for: requirements, Gantt charts, git graphs, kanban boards, mindmaps, timelines and user journeys.
