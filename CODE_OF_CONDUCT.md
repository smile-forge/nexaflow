# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Nexaflow to be a welcoming, friendly, and harassment-free project for
everyone — regardless of experience, background, identity, or skill level. As
contributors and maintainers, we pledge to keep participation in our community a
respectful and positive experience for all.

## What we expect

Behaviour that helps the community thrive:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful toward other people.
- Welcoming newcomers and offering constructive, good-faith feedback.
- Accepting differing viewpoints gracefully and focusing on what's best for the project.
- Showing empathy and assuming good intent.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel — and whenever someone is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately
to the maintainers. The simplest confidential channel is GitHub's private
reporting on this repository (**Security → Report a vulnerability**), which
reaches the maintainers without creating anything public. Please do **not** raise
conduct concerns in public issues.

All reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly and in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may take any
action they deem appropriate in response to behaviour that violates this Code of
Conduct — including editing or removing contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restricting a participant from the project.
