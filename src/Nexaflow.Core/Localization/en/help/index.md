# Nexaflow help

Every page in Nexaflow has a help page like this one: what the page is for, what you can do on it, and the
quickest way to do it. Help opens beside whatever you are working on and follows you as you move between tabs,
so what you are reading is always about what you are looking at.

- **Open or close help** with the **?** button above **Options**, or press **F1**.
- **Search** from the box at the top of this pane. Matches in the page you are reading light up first; every
  other help page that mentions it is listed underneath, ready to open.
- **Ask the assistant.** With help open, the AI bar knows what this page says, so "how do I…" questions are
  answered from it.
