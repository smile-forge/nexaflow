# Help

Explains the page you are working on, in a pane beside it.

---

## Opening and closing
- Press the ? button above Options, or F1. If the window isn't split, it splits: your page stays on the left and its help opens on the right. [Show me](locate:Chrome_HelpButton)
- If the window is already split, help opens in the pane beside the page you are using.
- Press ? or F1 again while help is showing that page and it closes. When nothing else is left in its pane, the split closes with it.

## Following the page you are on
- Click a tab in the other pane and help turns to that page's help.
- Once you move within help yourself — following a link, opening a search result, or choosing All help pages — it stays where you took it. Press ? to point it back at the page you are using.

## Moving around a page
- Every help page starts with a Topics list. Click a topic to jump to it.
- Each topic ends with a link back to that list.
- Links to other help pages open in this pane, some of them at the part they mean.

## Show me where
- A link with a pin on it points at a control on screen. Click it and a red lasso is drawn round that control for five seconds, or until you click.
- One link can point at several controls in turn: [the search box, then All help pages, then the ? button](locate:Help_SearchBox,Help_IndexButton,Chrome_HelpButton). Click, or wait, to move on to the next. The number on the lasso says how far through you are.
- Press Esc to stop.
- If what the link names isn't on screen — usually because its page isn't open beside help — the pane says so.

## Searching
- Type in the box at the top of the pane. [Show me](locate:Help_SearchBox)
- Matches in the page you are reading are highlighted and counted beside the box. Enter goes to the next match, Shift+Enter to the previous, Esc clears the search.
- Other help pages that match are listed under the box, most matches first. Click one to open it with the same search still running.
- Type `split pane` for lines with both words, `pan*` for words starting with pan, or `/F[0-9]+/` for a regular expression.

## All help pages
- The list button shows every help page there is. [Show me](locate:Help_IndexButton)
- A page that has no help of its own shows that list too, with a note saying so.

## Asking the assistant
- While help is open the assistant can see what it says. Ask "how do I…" in the bar at the bottom of the window and the answer comes from the help beside you.
- Type ? and a word in that bar to search help from there.
