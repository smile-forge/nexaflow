# Help

The help pane explains the page you are working on — what it is for, what you can do there, and how — in a pane
beside it, so the page and its explanation are both in view.

---

## Opening and closing help

Press the **?** button above **Options** in the top-right corner, or press **F1**.

- If the window isn't split, it splits: your page stays on the left and its help opens on the right.
- If the window is already split, help opens in the pane beside the page you are using.
- Press **?** or **F1** again while help is showing that page, and it closes. When its pane has nothing else in
  it, the split closes too.

## Help follows you

Click a tab in the other pane and help turns to that page's help. Nothing to press — what you are reading always
matches what you are looking at.

When you move within help yourself — following a link, opening a search result, or choosing **All help pages**
(the **☰** button) — help stays where you took it. Press **?** to point it back at the page you are using.

## Moving around a help page

Every help page opens with its **Topics**: click one to jump straight to it. Each topic ends with **↑ Back to
topics**, which takes you back to that list. Links to other help pages open in this pane, some of them straight at
the part they mean.

## Show me where

Some help points at the screen instead of describing it. A link with a pin on it, [like this one](locate:Chrome_HelpButton),
throws a red lasso round the control it means — there for five seconds, or until you click, whichever comes first.

One link can point at several things in turn: [the search box, then all help pages, then the ? button](locate:Help_SearchBox,Help_IndexButton,Chrome_HelpButton).
Click, or wait, and the lasso moves on to the next; the number on it says how far through you are. **Esc** stops it.

A "show me" link points at the window you are in, so what it names has to be on screen — usually because help is open
beside the page it belongs to. When it isn't, the pane says so rather than leaving you looking for a lasso.

## Searching

Type in the box at the top of the pane.

1. **This page first.** Every match in the help you are reading is highlighted, and the count appears beside the
   box. **Enter** moves to the next match, **Shift+Enter** to the previous one, and **Esc** clears the search.
2. **Then every other help page.** Pages that also match are listed under the box, the most matches first. Click
   one to open it; the same search carries over, so its matches light up straight away.

Searches understand more than plain words:

| You type | It finds |
|---|---|
| `split pane` | lines with both words |
| `pan*` | words starting with *pan* — pane, panes, panel |
| `/F[0-9]+/` | a regular expression: F1, F12… |

## All help pages

The **☰** button lists every help page there is. Pages with no help of their own yet show this list too, with a
note saying so.

## Asking the assistant

While help is open, the assistant can see what it says. Ask "how do I…" in the AI bar, and the answer comes from the
help beside you. Type `?` and a word to search help from the AI bar instead.
