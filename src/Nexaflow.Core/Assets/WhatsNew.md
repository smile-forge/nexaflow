# What's New in Nexaflow

Welcome to **Nexaflow 1.7** 🎉

This release is about *the page under your hand* — diagrams you can edit where they're drawn, help that points at the control it means, and Nexaflow in a second language.

### ✏️ Diagrams you can work in
*   Every diagram, formula, tune, barcode and chart in a markdown document is now drawn by **one engine**: click into it, select across it, and type where it's drawn.
*   **Flowchart** nodes can be renamed, relabelled, reshaped and recoloured in place, and a **pie** takes its legend, hole and stroke from the ribbon. Renames carry through — rename a branch in a git graph or an actor in a journey and every mention follows.
*   All **54 Mermaid flowchart shapes** draw as their own shape, and `img:` and `icon:` nodes draw too.
*   Right-click → **Insert** starts any of 41 kinds of block from a working example, so you don't need to know the syntax first.
*   A block's **Copy** puts a picture of it on the clipboard. A block that can't be read shows its source and why — and the rest of the document still draws.

### 🧬 New things to write
*   **`smiles`** — chemical structures from SMILES strings, with rings, stereo wedges and 3D cages.
*   **`abc`** — sheet music from ABC notation. LilyPond now engraves on the same engine.
*   **`wordcloud`** — words packed by weight, into a shape, a word's letters, or a picture's outline.
*   **`scatter`**, **`bubble`**, **`heatmap`** and **`density2d`** — correlation plots, with fit lines and statistics.

### 📖 Markdown, side by side
*   A **Rendered · Split · Source** slider puts the source beside the document, with a handle between them.
*   Links out of a document now open — web addresses in the browser, paths in their viewer.
*   Gantt charts and word clouds lay out in a fraction of the time.

### ❓ Help on every page
*   Press **?** (or **F1**) for help on the page you're on, opened beside it. It's searchable, and `?word` in the AI bar searches it too.
*   **"Show me where"** links draw a lasso round the control they describe, one step at a time.

### 🌍 Nexaflow in French
*   A **French** language pack — choose it under **Options → Language**.

### 🗂️ Files
*   A new **HDF5 viewer**, and `.h5` files open like folders, with their datasets as files you can copy out.
*   **Band-select** rows by dragging in empty space, and **right-drag** to be asked *Copy here* or *Move here*.
*   **Copy path** on the breadcrumb and on any folder in the tree.
*   **Save As** is one dialog that tells you what you'd replace and whether there's room, before you press Save.
*   Search looks **inside Word documents**, and inside PDFs in folders Windows doesn't index.

### 🎨 Your ribbon, your way
*   Pick from 5,700+ Fluent icons and emoji, and give each button its own colours and shape.

### ⚡ Smaller things you'll notice
*   The AI context panel shows thumbnails of the images in an image tab.
*   **Options → Network**: a kill switch, per-network sweep permission, and limits on what a scan may send.
*   A damaged settings file costs that setting, not the session — and lost workspaces are recovered from their folders.
*   Deleting the workspace a window is using moves the window instead of closing the app.
*   Ctrl+S saves in the JSON viewer.
*   Dialogs share one look, and Esc and Tab behave the same in all of them.
Thank you for being part of the journey. Press **Next** to continue.
